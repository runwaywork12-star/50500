# V24 — Day/night switch

The theme control is now located in the narrow announcement strip above the main navigation, beside the contact number.

- Dark storefront: click the small switch to open `/v2` (light theme).
- Light storefront: click it to return to `/` (dark theme).
- Sun and moon icons cross-fade with a subtle glow pulse.
- The control remains visible on mobile; the phone number may hide on small screens.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables the blinking/pulse animations.

No new dependencies or database migrations are required.
